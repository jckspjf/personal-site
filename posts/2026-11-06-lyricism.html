''Can I open up, is it safe, or not? I'm afraid a little, you relate, or not?''

As the first 'proper' post here, I think I'll start with something that I think is deeply important although often ignored. I think a lot of true meaning behind popular songs is often missed, even when it's obvious.

For example, and it's the key point of this post, the following lyrics:

Can I open up, is it safe, or not? I'm afraid a little, you relate, or not?

- Die Hard, by Kendrick Lamar

I think that these lyrics directly correlate to my main goal here: these are something you hear directly, and often overlook when listening as a more casual or background listener, while the actual meaning in here is shown in vulnerability and being honest & real to yourself.

I know this isn't the typical length, for me personally, but I think it's a good basis for further, more nuanced posts that go deeper into specific topics of beliefs at that time.
